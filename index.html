Plan Foundation

Intent & Context

What this plan is for and why now

Your clients get MTD compliant. You get your time back while I plan, organise and coordinate the compliance setup process from start to finish.

Core Value Proposition — Michele Archer

Plan Intent

This is a 90-day lead generation marketing plan for a freelance MTD compliance support service targeting small accountancy practices, initially in Sussex with a view to scaling UK-wide. The plan is time-sensitive, with HMRC's MTD for sole traders deadline of April 2026 creating genuine urgency for accountancy practices with non-compliant clients. The goal is to convert one warm contact into a confirmed paid engagement within 45–60 days, while moving a second contact into active conversation. Longer-term visibility will be built through organic LinkedIn outreach and local networking.

Why Now

HMRC's Making Tax Digital rollout creates a hard compliance deadline for sole traders. Accountancy practices face the challenge of managing this for their clients without the internal resource to do it profitably. This plan positions Michele Archer as the specialist who removes that burden entirely, allowing practices to protect client relationships without diverting fee-generating capacity.

Strategic Approach

Framework Selection

Why the 7Ps and what was considered

Selected Framework: The 7Ps Marketing Mix

The 7Ps framework was selected as the most appropriate structure for this plan. As a service business, the extended marketing mix (Product, Price, Place, Promotion, People, Process, Physical Evidence) captures both the strategic and operational dimensions of the offer. It is broad enough to be comprehensive without being so complex that it produces shallow coverage across too many areas.

Considered but rejected

STP Model

Useful for audience definition but limited to segmentation and positioning. Does not address service delivery elements, which are a key part of the competitive advantage in a hands-on compliance support role.

Considered but rejected

SOSTAC

A comprehensive planning framework well suited to larger campaigns. Too extensive for a focused 90-day plan with a restricted budget. The risk of producing shallow work across too many areas outweighed the benefit of its structure.

Marketing Mix

The 7Ps

How the service is positioned and delivered across all dimensions

P1

Product

A freelance MTD compliance coordination service for accountancy practices. The service covers planning, organising, and coordinating the MTD setup process for sole trader clients on behalf of the practice. The accountant retains oversight while Michele manages the end-to-end compliance journey, freeing the practice from the administrative burden without losing control of the client relationship.

P2

Price

Pricing will be structured as a per-client engagement fee or a monthly retained arrangement depending on the volume of clients a practice needs to process. Specific pricing will be developed in discussion with the first confirmed client to ensure it reflects genuine value and is commercially viable for both parties. The pricing model will be positioned as a cost-effective alternative to the practice absorbing the time burden internally.

P3

Place

Primary channels for the 90-day period are:

  • Direct outreach to two warm Sussex-based contacts
  • LinkedIn outreach targeting accountancy practices in Sussex and beyond
  • Local professional networking events in Sussex
  • Organic LinkedIn content to build broader UK visibility
P4

Promotion

Three distinct messaging approaches aligned to audience temperature (see Messaging section). Promotion is entirely organic and relationship-led within the 90-day plan, supported by a small LinkedIn investment. Content strategy uses MTD deadline awareness as a natural hook, positioning Michele as an informed, reliable specialist rather than a salesperson.

P5

People

Michele Archer brings a background in organisation, coordination, and reliable execution. Her operating style — plan-first, steady consistent delivery — directly mirrors the reassurance an accountant needs when handing off a compliance process. The service is personal, relationship-led, and built on trust rather than scale.

P6

Process

The handoff process will be clearly defined for each practice engagement, covering how client information is received, how progress is communicated, and how completion is confirmed. A clear process reduces the perceived risk for the accountant and demonstrates professionalism from the first conversation. The process will be documented and refined after the first client engagement.

P7

Physical Evidence

In a service business, physical evidence is what reassures a prospective client that you are credible before they have direct experience of your work. For this plan, physical evidence comprises: an optimised LinkedIn profile clearly communicating the MTD compliance specialism; this marketing plan as a demonstration of structured thinking and professionalism; and, once the first engagement is complete, a brief case study or testimonial to support further outreach. The personal brand page serves as a digital anchor for all outreach activity.

Communication Strategy

Messaging Versions

Three approaches aligned to audience temperature and MTD timeline relevance

Version 1 — Warm Contacts

Urgency-Led

For the two existing warm contacts where a relationship already exists and the April 2026 deadline is immediately relevant. Messaging acknowledges the known challenge directly and moves quickly to a practical conversation about working together. Tone is confident and collegial, not salesy.

Version 2 — Cold Prospects, Near-Term

Education-Led

For accountancy practices not yet engaged but who have sole trader clients affected by the April 2026 deadline. Messaging leads with value and awareness rather than urgency pressure. Positions Michele as an informed specialist worth a conversation before the deadline crunch hits.

Version 3 — Future Threshold Prospects

Awareness-Led

For practices whose clients fall under later MTD implementation thresholds. No immediate urgency but a clear opportunity to build visibility and plant seeds for future engagement. Content positions Michele as the go-to name for MTD compliance coordination when the next wave of deadlines becomes relevant.

Bias Note

Messaging has been deliberately designed to avoid assumptions about practice size, ownership profile, or geography. Outreach will include practices of varying scale and demographic profile, including female-led practices and larger firms with proportionally higher compliance burdens, not only the small independent practice profile that informed the initial plan.

Budget Rationale

The budget reflects the reality of marketing an unvalidated service offer with restricted income. The priority is testing demand at low cost rather than committing significant spend before market response is understood. The largest single allocation (networking) reflects the judgment that relationship-led outreach will outperform digital advertising at this stage of the business. All figures have been verified against current pricing. Any AI-suggested costs were checked before inclusion.

90-Day Action Plan

Timeline

Phased activity from February to May 2026

1

Days 1–14 — Foundation

Optimise LinkedIn profile to clearly communicate MTD compliance specialism. Draft outreach messages using Version 1 (urgency-led) for both warm contacts. Make direct contact with warmer lead and open a practical conversation about working together. Begin publishing organic LinkedIn content using MTD deadline awareness as the primary hook.

2

Days 15–30 — Outreach

Follow up with warm contacts and progress conversations toward a discovery call or proposal. Activate LinkedIn Sales Navigator for targeted cold outreach using Version 2 (education-led) messaging. Identify and register for first Sussex networking event. Continue weekly LinkedIn content.

3

Day 30 — Review Checkpoint

Honest assessment of traction. Are warm contacts progressing? Is LinkedIn generating responses? If signals are weak, revisit messaging before continuing outreach. If engagement is positive, maintain course. This checkpoint is built in to avoid continuing ineffective activity through the full 90 days.

  • If warm contacts are stalling: address the specific blocker directly
  • If LinkedIn outreach is getting limited response: refine messaging based on real feedback
  • If needed: widen outreach to larger firms and female-led practices identified in the bias review
4

Days 31–60 — Conversion Focus

Target period for confirming first paid engagement (45–60 day goal). Attend first Sussex networking event. Continue cold outreach at steady pace. Move second warm contact into active proposal conversation. Begin gathering any early case study material from first engagement if confirmed.

5

Days 61–90 — Scale and Learn

With first engagement underway, use the experience to refine the process and strengthen physical evidence (case study, testimonial). Broaden LinkedIn outreach to UK-wide prospects using Version 3 (awareness-led) messaging for future threshold audiences. Assess whether continued LinkedIn Premium investment is justified based on results to date.

Measuring Success

Success Metrics

Realistic targets for the 90-day period

1
Confirmed Paid Engagement

At least one accountancy practice confirmed as a paying client within 45–60 days. Primary target is the warmer of the two existing contacts.

1
Active Proposal Conversation

Second warm contact in active discussion with a proposal or scoping conversation underway by day 60.

3+
New Prospect Connections

At least three new accountancy practice connections established through LinkedIn or networking with meaningful engagement initiated.

Contingency: If Targets Are Not Met at Day 30

If warm contacts are not progressing and cold outreach is generating minimal response by day 30, the plan will adapt rather than continue unchanged. Options include refining messaging based on real responses, widening the audience profile to larger practices and female-led firms, and considering whether a free initial consultation offer would reduce the barrier to a first conversation. The goal is learning from the market quickly, not optimistic persistence.

Responsible Practice

Ethics & Responsible Use

Data privacy, AI use, and transparency decisions

Data Privacy

All information shared with AI tools during this project has been limited to client profile types, business scenarios, and market characteristics. No specific accountant names, practice details, client information, or commercially sensitive data has been entered into any AI platform. This approach naturally anonymises inputs while still enabling AI to provide genuinely useful support.

Transparency with Contacts

Both warm contacts will be informed that a formal marketing plan and business approach is being developed, and that our conversations have informed the understanding of the market need. No specific details of those discussions are included in any documentation shared externally without their knowledge. This transparency is also expected to reinforce professionalism and seriousness of intent.

Bias Review

The initial plan was reviewed for bias risks before drafting. Two corrections were made: the target audience was broadened beyond the small independent practice profile to include larger firms and female-led practices; and messaging was structured in three versions to avoid applying urgency pressure indiscriminately across audiences at different stages of MTD awareness.

Accuracy and Verification

All figures in this plan, including MTD deadlines, LinkedIn pricing, and networking event costs, have been verified against current sources. AI-suggested costs were checked before inclusion rather than accepted at face value. Marketing claims in outreach messaging will only reference facts that can be supported. No unverified market size statistics or conversion rate claims are included in this plan.
